Teaching

Courses taught by Prof. Jung Heon Lee in the School of Advanced Materials Science & Engineering and the Department of MetaBioHealth.

4courses
2levels
Graduate

Bio-integrated Materials

The objective of this class is to provide an overview of the recent contribution of science and engineering in biological systems and medicine with an emphasis on materials.

Undergraduate

Biomaterials

This class is designed for material scientists to learn the basics of organic chemistry, biochemistry, and biophysics for biological applications. General properties of biomolecules (such as nucleic acids, protein, and lipid), biological systems (such as cells), and their integration with materials will be covered.

Undergraduate

Mechanical Properties of Advanced Materials

This class is aimed for materials scientists to understand the relationship between the microstructure and mechanical properties of materials and to design materials with excellent mechanical properties.

Undergraduate

Experiments in Advanced Materials Processing

The objective of this class to provide an opportunity for students to experience several important processes in materials fields.
